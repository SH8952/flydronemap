LiPo batteries degrade with every charge cycle, but how you store, charge, and use them significantly affects how many usable cycles you actually get.

## Key takeaways

- For storage, manufacturers recommend a partial charge rather than full or empty. DJI's Mini 4 Pro manual says 50% to 60%, and other models differ, so check yours.
- Charge only within the allowed temperature range and let a battery cool to room temperature after flight first.
- Never use a swollen, leaking or damaged battery.
- DJI rates several of its consumer batteries for about 200 cycles and recommends periodic full charges during long storage.

## Storage charge, not full charge

For a battery that will not be flown within a day or two, avoid storing it full or empty. The DJI Mini 4 Pro maintenance manual recommends storage at 50% to 60% remaining charge and warns that storing a fully charged or empty battery will seriously damage its life. Other models give different guidance, which is why the manual matters:

| Source | Storage guidance |
|---|---|
| DJI Mini 4 Pro maintenance manual | 50% to 60% remaining charge; fully charge once every three months. |
| DJI Mavic Air battery safety guidelines | Discharge to 40% to 65% if it will not be used for 10 days or more; do not store after fully discharging. |
| DJI Mini 4K user manual | Discharge to 30% or lower during transport or storage; recommended storage temperature of 22°C to 28°C for periods over three months. |
| DJI Mavic 3 user manual | Batteries discharge automatically to 96% after three days idle and to 60% after nine days; fully charge at least once every three months. |

Many DJI batteries have this self-discharge function, so you often do not have to manage storage level by hand. Even so, do not leave a battery in a drained state for months.

## Avoid extreme temperatures, both directions

Do not store or charge batteries in a hot car, direct sun or freezing conditions. The Mavic Air guidelines give an ideal storage temperature of 22°C to 28°C and say never to store batteries above 45°C or below 0°C. The Mavic 3 Enterprise guidelines say not to leave batteries near heat sources, under direct sunlight, or in a vehicle in hot weather. See [extreme heat: drone battery and electronics precautions](/en/guides/extreme-heat-drone-battery-electronics-precautions) and temperature and drone battery performance for use in the extremes.

## Do not fly a battery down to zero

Discharging a LiPo deeply stresses the cells. The Mavic Air and Mavic 3 Enterprise guidelines both warn against storing a battery for an extended period after full discharge because it may over-discharge and cause irreparable damage. Land with a reasonable reserve. The manuals cited here do not specify a percentage for it, so a personal reserve is your own risk decision; use your aircraft's low-battery and return-to-home settings as a floor, not a target. See [return-to-home settings explained](/en/guides/return-to-home-settings-explained).

## Let batteries cool before charging

DJI says not to charge an Intelligent Flight Battery immediately after flight because its temperature may be too high, and to wait until it cools to near room temperature. Charge within the 5°C to 40°C range given in the Mavic 3 and Mini 4K manuals; the Mavic 3 Enterprise guidelines describe 22°C to 28°C as ideal. The Mini 4K manual adds that charging stops automatically if the battery exceeds 50°C.

## Inspect regularly

Before every flight, check for swelling, punctures, leaks or a case that no longer sits flat, and that the battery is seated securely. The Mini 4 Pro maintenance manual says a damaged, swollen, leaking or flooded battery may rupture or catch fire and must not be used or transported, including for repair or replacement shipping. The Mavic 3 Enterprise guidelines say to contact DJI or an authorized dealer in such cases. A swollen battery should be retired, not used carefully.

## Respect the cycle rating

The Mini 4 Pro maintenance manual states the battery is rated for 200 cycles and should not be used beyond that, and the Mini 4K manual says continued use afterward is not recommended. Ratings differ by model, so find yours and track how many times each battery has been charged. If your aircraft's app shows a cycle count, use it.

## Rotate your batteries

If you own several batteries, rotate them so wear stays roughly even across the set. Marking batteries with numbers and logging their cycles makes this easy. This is common practice rather than a manufacturer requirement.

## Traveling with batteries

Airline rules for spare lithium batteries are strict. The FAA states that spare lithium-ion batteries must be in carry-on baggage only, are limited to 100 Wh per battery, with up to two spare larger batteries of 101 to 160 Wh allowed with airline approval, and that terminals must be protected from short circuit. Damaged or recalled batteries must not be carried aboard. Rules differ by country and airline, so check before you travel and see [packing a drone travel bag checklist](/en/guides/packing-a-drone-travel-bag-checklist).

## Common mistakes

- Leaving batteries fully charged for weeks.
- Charging right after a flight or in a hot car.
- Continuing to use a puffy battery.
- Storing a flat battery for months.
- Ignoring the cycle rating.

## Quick care checklist

- Store at the charge level your manual recommends, in a cool, dry place out of sun.
- Fully charge periodically during long storage if your manual says so.
- Inspect before every flight and retire damaged batteries.
- Cool before charging; charge within the allowed range.
- Check wind and temperature for your site on the [FlyDroneMap dashboard](/en) before heading out.

## FAQ

### What charge level should I store my drone battery at?

It depends on the model. DJI's Mini 4 Pro manual says 50% to 60%, while its Mini 4K manual says 30% or lower for storage or transport, so follow your own manual.

### How long should I wait before charging after a flight?

DJI does not give a fixed time; it says to wait until the battery has cooled to near room temperature. If it still feels warm, wait longer.

### How many cycles does a drone battery last?

DJI rates the Mini 4 Pro battery for 200 cycles and says not to use it beyond that. Other models have different ratings.

### Can I take spare drone batteries on a plane?

The FAA allows spare lithium-ion batteries in carry-on baggage only, up to 100 Wh each, with limited larger batteries by airline approval. Always check your airline and country rules first.
