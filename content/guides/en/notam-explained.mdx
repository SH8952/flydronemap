A NOTAM (Notice to Air Missions, formerly Notice to Airmen) is an official notice that alerts pilots to information affecting a specific airspace or airport's operations. That can include runway closures, navigation aid outages, a newly established TFR, laser light shows, and much more.

## How it relates to a TFR

A TFR is one specific type of NOTAM. In other words, every TFR is a NOTAM, but not every NOTAM is a TFR — plenty of NOTAMs (like runway construction) have nothing to do with drone flights.

## What drone pilots should know

- Many countries, including the US, offer a free official NOTAM lookup system (the FAA's NOTAM Search in the US).
- If you're planning to fly near an airport or airfield, it's worth checking for any active NOTAMs in that radius before you launch.
- Some commercial drone flight-planning apps, including B4UFLY, surface relevant NOTAMs automatically.
- NOTAMs are often written in dense aviation shorthand, which can look intimidating at first — but just checking whether anything applies to your location and time window already goes a long way.
